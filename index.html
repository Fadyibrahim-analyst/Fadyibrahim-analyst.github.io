About

I help small businesses, e-commerce brands, and retailers make sense of their data — without the jargon or the wait. My background is in operations and supply chain at Domanza, where I led inventory reporting for a four-person team and helped cut stockouts by 70%. That's where I learned how messy real business data actually is, and how much clearer decisions become once it's organized.
